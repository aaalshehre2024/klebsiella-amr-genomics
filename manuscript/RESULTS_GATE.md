# Results drafting gate

Begin final numerical Results only after the collection manifest and evidence eligibility are approved. Interim accession counts are audit findings, not a biological sample-size claim.

Planned order: cohort construction and evidence coverage; observed joint ST–K/O–carbapenemase profiles; within-lineage resistance architecture; explicitly defined resistance–virulence co-detection; verified tree mapping; sensitivity to duplicates, uncertain calls, missing output layers and snapshot choice.

Each final claim must cite its generating script, input checksum, output table, denominator and manuscript figure/table. No values are to be anticipated or filled from earlier chat summaries. MDR and virulence marker co-detection are not clinical resistance or hypervirulence validation. Separate exploratory decisions from genuinely prespecified rules.
